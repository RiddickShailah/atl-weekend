Friday, Oct 9

Outdoor day
  1. 1:30 to 5:00 PM Hike Stone Mountain

    The walk-up trail is about a mile each way on steep granite. Do it today, since the rock gets slick in the rain.

    Free hike, $20 parking per car. 25 to 30 min drive. Directions

  2. 6:30 PM Dinner at Colony Square

    Free to walk around, pay for food. In Midtown. Directions

  3. Evening, optional CoolFit 5K Neon Night Run/Walk

    Free. Check the event listing for the start location.

  4. 11:55 PM The 420 Friday Comedy Show

    At the Atlanta Comedy Theater in Norcross.

    Tickets from about $37. 25 to 30 min drive. Directions

Saturday, Oct 10

Rain day, stay inside
  1. 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park

    Get there at 9 to grab Birth Home tour spots. What to see

    Free, with free parking. About 10 min drive. Directions

  2. 12:30 PM Lunch at Sweet Auburn Curb Market

    A covered market about 5 min from the MLK park. Directions

  3. 2:00 to 4:30 PM World of Coca-Cola

    Veterans and service members get one free general admission ticket.

    Adults $24.95 to $27.95, seniors $22.95 to $25.95, kids 3 to 12 $20.95 to $23.95. About 10 min drive. Directions

  4. 5:30 PM Shopping and dinner at Lenox Square

    Free to visit. 15 to 20 min drive, or MARTA to Lenox station. Directions

  5. If the rain stops Atlanta Pride Festival

    Free, 10 AM to 10 PM in Piedmont Park. Take MARTA; streets nearby are closed. Directions

Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park

Plan 2 to 3 hours. Everything here is free.

Hours
Daily, 9 AM to 5 PM
Parking
Free lot at 423 John Wesley Dobbs Ave NE
Start here
The visitor center, temporarily at Historic Fire Station No. 6, 39 Boulevard NE. Pick up Birth Home tour tickets here.
Birth Home
501 Auburn Ave NE, reopened in summer 2026. You can only go inside on a free ranger tour. Tours hold 15 people and tickets are first come, first served on the same day.
Ebenezer Baptist Church
Where Dr. King and his father preached. Rangers lead short Church Talks daily.
The King Center
Dr. and Mrs. King's tomb, the Eternal Flame and Freedom Hall
Also nearby
The International Civil Rights Walk of Fame and the Peace Rose Garden
Guided tour
Unexpected Atlanta Tours runs a 2.5-hour walking tour (about 1.7 miles, rain or shine). It doesn't go inside the Birth Home. Book ahead.

Rainy-day picks for the family

All indoors. Book timed tickets online, since rainy weekends sell out.

  • Georgia AquariumWhale sharks, dolphins and sea lions
  • World of Coca-ColaThe tasting room is a kid favorite
  • Children's Museum of AtlantaHands-on play for ages 8 and under
  • Fernbank MuseumDinosaurs and giant-screen films
  • College Football Hall of FameInteractive exhibits and an indoor field
  • Center for Puppetry ArtsPuppet shows and a museum, in Midtown
  • LEGO Discovery CenterAt Phipps Plaza, best for ages 3 to 10
  • High Museum of ArtThe I SPY! exhibit is made for families
  • Center for Civil and Human RightsPairs well with the MLK park for teens
  • Delta Flight MuseumPlanes and a flight simulator
