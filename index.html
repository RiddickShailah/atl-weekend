Traffic heads-up

Pride weekend, a concert, a Falcons game and an I-285 closure all land on the same weekend. Leave extra time.

  • Fri, 4 to 7 PM
    Rush hourThe Downtown Connector (I-75/85) and GA-400 back up. Plan any cross-town drives before 4 or after 7.
  • Fri 7 PM to Mon 5 AM
    I-285 fully closed on the west sideBetween South Atlanta Rd (Exit 16) and South Cobb Dr (Exit 15). Avoid it if you're coming in from the west or northwest. GDOT may cancel it if the weather is bad.
  • Sat and Sun, all day
    Pride Festival at Piedmont ParkHeavy traffic and street closures around the park in Midtown. Take MARTA or a rideshare in that area.
  • Sat, from about 5 PM
    Weezer at State Farm Arena (7 PM)Downtown fills up near World of Coca-Cola. Leave Downtown by 5.
  • Sat, all day
    RainWet roads mean slower traffic everywhere. Add 10 to 15 minutes to every drive.
  • Sun, noon to about 5 PM
    Pride Parade closes Peachtree StPeachtree is closed from Civic Center MARTA up to 10th St, and 10th St stays closed about an hour after the parade. From church to Ponce City Market, take I-75/85 to North Ave instead.
  • Sun, from late afternoon
    Falcons vs. Ravens (8:20 PM)Downtown and the Connector get busy before kickoff. Coming back from Stone Mountain, head home before 6 PM or expect delays on I-20 near Downtown.

Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park

Saturday morning, weather permitting. Plan 2 to 3 hours. Everything here is free.

Hours
Daily, 9 AM to 5 PM
Parking
Free lot at 423 John Wesley Dobbs Ave NE
Start here
The visitor center, temporarily at Historic Fire Station No. 6, 39 Boulevard NE. Pick up Birth Home tour tickets here.
Birth Home
501 Auburn Ave NE, reopened in summer 2026. You can only go inside on a free ranger tour. Tours hold 15 people and tickets are first come, first served on the same day.
Ebenezer Baptist Church
Where Dr. King and his father preached. Rangers lead short Church Talks daily.
The King Center
Dr. and Mrs. King's tomb, the Eternal Flame and Freedom Hall
Also nearby
The International Civil Rights Walk of Fame and the Peace Rose Garden
Guided tour
Unexpected Atlanta Tours runs a 2.5-hour walking tour (about 1.7 miles, rain or shine). It doesn't go inside the Birth Home. Book ahead.
